🎯 Future Leaders Experience Program

📍 Government High School, Chitana, Sonipat

📅 22nd August 2026

🎯 Self-Awareness, Self-Management & Stress Management

👥 30 Students | Classes 9–10

⏱️ 2-Hour Interactive Session

Embrace Learning conducted a 2-hour Future Leaders Experience Program for students of Classes 9 and 10 at Government High School, Chitana, Sonipat. The session focused on self-awareness, self-management, handling emotions, and stress management. Through interactive activities such as Self Introduction, Handling Emotions, and the Habit Ladder, students reflected on their thoughts, emotions, habits, and everyday choices.

"The session provided students with an opportunity to pause, reflect, and better understand themselves while developing practical skills for managing emotions, habits, and everyday challenges."

— School Incharge
